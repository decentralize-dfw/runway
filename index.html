C2W2 — 2026 Experience

From Touch to Click

A 30-minute browser-based runway experience featuring 48 avatars across six thematic chapters, exploring the evolution from physical touch to digital click—the new biology of human existence.

Touch, the touch of Adam, or the message that came to Muhammad through the act of reading. These are gestures that appeal to the senses, forming the foundation of being human. At the end of touch, reading, wars, inventions, kingdoms, nations, stories—humankind has evolved into the era of the click.

Knowledge once acquired through touch is now absorbed by tapping a digital screen or clicking a mouse. With a single click you can earn billions, lose everything, reconstruct yourself from scratch. The same applies to agents—with a click, we generate an identity, a character, nearly an entire life.

Click to wear, click to be. The phrase is a pun on "ready to wear"—a digital reinterpretation. Yet the deeper meaning of the click lies in its resonance with biology: just as cells divide, a tree grows, a flower blooms, clicking is the digital counterpart of a natural process.

Chapter 01
Evolution of Medium of Traditional Art

The runway begins with the avatarization of painting—one of the most traditional mediums. Avatars inspired by masters like Francis Bacon and Matisse, designed with the aid of AI, transform classic art into wearable digital beings. This is the moment when painting stops being a surface and becomes a body, an identity walking through virtual space.

Chapter 02
Virtual Fashion — Just Design

Pure digital fashion design takes center stage. The chapter explores variations of virtual fashion on "flat" avatars, probing their limits and experimenting with different personalities. No physical constraints, no material costs—only the boundaries of imagination and code. This is fashion freed from gravity, texture unrestricted by thread count.

Chapter 03
Evolution of Medium of Art as NFT

A hybrid of the first two chapters—a historical continuation where NFTs and notable figures merge with digital design. Familiar faces recontextualized reduce the "coolness" of mere spectacle, pushing the viewer into meaning itself. This is where digital ownership meets identity, where blockchain authentication becomes part of the aesthetic.

Chapter 04
AI-Powered Design

Much like what the internet and AI did to us initially, this chapter resets everything. Through avatars generated entirely by AI, the runway erases and devalues what came before. This mirrors the cheapening of "cool visuals" under the flood of AI-produced imagery, where hand-made labor loses its aura. The disruption is intentional, uncomfortable, necessary.

Chapter 05
Transhumanism

The chapter sanctifies, in a minimalist way, the identity-dissolving nature of these new design possibilities—both for the designer and the user. Drawing on small references from earlier chapters, it stages a parametric, historical ascension, like the most sacred of avatars or the salvation of humanity itself. Alone after material bombardment, searching for meaning.

Chapter 06
Evolution of Art as Mutant Meaning

No new avatars appear. Like a phoenix rising from ashes, the runway closes with rebirth. Strategically selected avatars from previous chapters return, altered—recognizable yet transformed. Not nostalgia, but a fragmented transformation of a simulated century condensed into thirty minutes. The core theme: AI, metaverse, blockchain, belonging, identity.

Browser-Based

Built with Three.js and WebGL. No downloads, no apps. Click a link and you're inside a 3D space. Works on desktop, mobile, VR headsets—same assets, same quality, same choreography across all platforms.

On-Chain Forever

The entire experience is minted as a single HTML NFT on Ethereum with all assets stored on Arweave. Platform-independent, permanent, owned. The first full metaverse runway experience on-chain.

Ultra-Realistic Physics

Garments created in Clo3D with textile physics simulating real fabric movement in real-time. Each garment averages 5MB, optimized to run smoothly on standard devices. Dynamic lighting shifts every 30 seconds.

Agent Interfaces

These avatars aren't just art—they're prototypes for the future where everyone has AI agents, and those agents need digital bodies. C2W2 avatars can be linked to agent applications, assigned tasks, placed in metaverse environments.

In today's digitized world—where algorithms have become the new governments and regulators—it has become nearly impossible to distinguish the real from the fake. We are heading toward a world where every person and company will have an agent for each task, and those agents will communicate with other agents.

Just as businesses have websites and individuals have social accounts, these agents must have interfaces on the internet. Their interfaces are avatars. The difference with C2W2 avatars lies in their design and philosophy: they emerge entirely from this paradigm shift.

When the experience ends, the feelings the audience carries remain beyond code, with no biological equivalent yet written. Just as an AI that recommends meditation cannot meditate itself, the impressions left in the viewer are organic, immeasurable. They can only click, and watch.

C2W — 2023 Experience

Click to Wear

The first virtual runway. Launched November 2023 on Hyperfy metaverse platform. A six-week residency exploring fashion and digital realms, featuring 16 avatar designs presented in a token-gated live event.

C2W (Click-to-Wear) was the beginning. November 2023. A collaboration with VCA x DRAUP, a six-week virtual residency exploring the intersection of fashion and digital realms. The first proof that virtual runways could work.

60 avatars were designed. 16 were selected for the launch runway and New Codes exhibition at Royal Institution, London. The event was token-gated—$20 tickets purchased between November 1-2, show on November 9. Attendees received "The Viewer," a complimentary avatar they could showcase during the event.

The runway was hosted on Hyperfy, a browser-based metaverse platform. No downloads required, no VR headset necessary. Just click a link and enter. People joined from different countries, experienced the same moment together, owned their participation through blockchain-verified tickets.

First Virtual Runway

C2W proved that virtual fashion shows could deliver high-quality presentations with global accessibility. No venue costs, no seating capacity limits, no geography barriers. Just a browser and internet connection.

Token-Gated Access

Attendees purchased tickets as NFTs for $20, creating both exclusivity and proof of attendance. Each ticket holder received "The Viewer" avatar as a wearable digital identity for the event.

New Codes Exhibition

16 selected avatars were showcased at the New Codes exhibition at Royal Institution, London. Bridging virtual and physical spaces, demonstrating that digital fashion has real-world cultural presence.

RedDAO Recognition

Won 1st place from RedDAO members at the Digital Fashion Residency Exhibition, standing out among 22 artists. Industry validation that virtual runways represent the future of fashion presentation.

The C2W virtual runway on Hyperfy was accessible via VR or PC through the link hyperfy.io/c2w. It showcased digital fashion pieces in a minimalist, dark environment with dramatic lighting that shifted every 30 seconds, presenting a seamless narrative through a single, cohesive concept.

Each avatar design explored different themes—some inspired by traditional art mediums transformed into digital beings, others purely experimental virtual fashion pushing the boundaries of what clothing could mean in digital space.

The show wasn't just about displaying garments. It was a proof of concept: fashion shows don't need physical venues, exclusive guest lists, or million-dollar budgets. They need vision, technology, and accessibility. C2W demonstrated all three.

C2W was the prototype. When Hyperfy v1 shut down, it became clear that platform dependency was a weakness. The lesson: virtual experiences need permanence, independence, ownership.

That lesson led to C2W2—rebuilt from scratch using pure Three.js code, minted as an on-chain NFT, stored on Arweave forever. C2W proved virtual runways could work. C2W2 proved they could be permanent.

Philosophy

Digital Space as Sovereign Territory

Virtual runways aren't simulations of physical shows. They're something that can only exist digitally—a complete reimagining of how fashion can be presented, experienced, and owned.

Our philosophy stems from the same principle as Religious Robotics: let digital space be what it actually is, not a simulation of physical reality. Traditional fashion shows are beautiful, but they're bound by gravity, material costs, venue capacity, linear time.

Virtual runways embrace digital space's true nature. Garments can float, transform, respond to sound. Viewers can teleport, see from impossible angles, experience the same moment from infinite viewpoints. The runway becomes a living architectural space where fashion, light, sound, and movement compose a total artwork.

We design with this freedom in mind. Fabrics that couldn't exist physically—chrome textures with impossible draping, translucent materials that shift with light, parametric patterns that animate based on viewer position. The avatars themselves are not trying to be realistic humans; they're digital beings designed for digital existence.

No Gravity

Fashion freed from physical constraints. Garments float, materials behave impossibly, structures defy architecture. Design for weightlessness, not runway walks.

Infinite Capacity

Every seat is the best seat because there are no seats. Global accessibility without venue limitations. Anyone with internet can attend from anywhere.

Permanent Ownership

On-chain storage means the experience exists forever. Not just recorded—actually preserved. Blockchain authentication ensures provenance and ownership.

Spatial Narrative

Runways as three-dimensional storytelling. Multi-layered experiences where choreography, lighting, sound, and interaction compose meaning beyond mere garment display.
